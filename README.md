# Europe-Ready CV

Turn your existing CV into three versions for jobs in Europe, free and in the browser:

1. **Europass-style CV** with the full CEFR language grid (listening, reading, spoken production, spoken interaction, writing)
2. **ATS-friendly CV**: plain and single-column, so job-portal robots can read it
3. **German Lebenslauf**: the tabular layout with personal data, an optional photo and an *Ort, Datum / Unterschrift* line

**[Open the tool](https://tonymathewz.github.io/europe-ready-cv/)** (no sign-up, no payment)

## How it works
1. **Bring in your CV.** Drop a PDF, Word (.docx) or text file, or paste the text. The tool reads it and fills in the form: name, contact details, jobs with dates, education, languages and skills.
2. **Check and complete it.** Automatic reading is never perfect, so you review every section. Add language levels, a photo if you want one, and a driving licence.
3. **Download.** Get a PDF (print dialog, Save as PDF) or a Word file (.doc) in any of the three formats.

Use **Save my data** to download your details as a small file, and **Load data** to carry on editing later or on another computer.

## Privacy
- Everything runs in your browser. Your CV is **never uploaded** to a server.
- Your details are kept in this browser's local storage so they survive a refresh. Use **Start over** to clear them.
- To read **PDF** files, the page loads the open-source [pdf.js](https://mozilla.github.io/pdf.js/) library from a public CDN. Word and text files need no internet at all.

## Limits
- Scanned PDFs (photos of paper) contain no text, so they can't be read. Paste or type the text instead.
- The reader works best with a clear CV that has headings like *Experience*, *Education*, *Languages* and *Skills*, and dates like `03/2021 - Present` or `2018 - 2022`.
- "Europass" is a trademark of the European Union. This tool makes Europass-*style* CVs and is not connected to the EU or the official [Europass](https://europass.europa.eu/) service.

## Why I built it
I paid for a Europass conversion when I first applied for jobs abroad. Many people from India, Africa and Asia applying to European jobs face the same re-typing, so this is free.
